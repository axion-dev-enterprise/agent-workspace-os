# Creative templates

These templates work with Canva, Runway, local editing tools, and a manual
review process. Canva supports brand-template filling, social resizing, and
image/video export; see the [Canva developer overview](https://www.canva.dev/docs/).
Runway capability and credit availability must be checked in the connected
workspace before a media-generation request.

## Campaign brief

```text
Objective:
Audience / market:
Offer and proof:
Primary platform(s):
Desired action / CTA:
Brand guardrails (logo, colours, type, prohibited claims):
Source assets and their usage rights:
Deliverables (for example: 1 hero, 3 stories, 1 reel cover):
Approval owner:
Publish window and destination account:
```

## Social creative matrix

| Asset | Role | Core message | Visual direction | CTA | Review state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hero post | Awareness | One promise | Product / founder / 3D scene | Learn more | Draft |
| Story or Reel | Consideration | Hook in first seconds | Motion + captions | Swipe / visit | Draft |
| Carousel | Education | One point per panel | Consistent grid | Save / share | Draft |
| Ad variant | Conversion | Claim + proof | High-contrast product frame | Shop / contact | Draft |

Make one approved master composition before resizing. Preserve safe margins
and re-check captions after every crop.

## Canva handoff

```text
Create from: [master design or brand template]
Brand kit: [name]
Variants: [platform + aspect ratio]
Locked elements: [logo / legal / price]
Editable elements: [headline / CTA / image slot]
Export: [PNG, JPG, MP4]
Destination: review folder first; do not publish until approved.
```

## Runway generation prompt

```text
Create a [duration] [aspect-ratio] social video for [audience].
Subject: [product/person/object and supplied reference].
Action: [single visible action].
Art direction: [lighting, lens language, colour palette, material].
Composition: reserve clean space at [top/bottom/left/right] for captions.
Motion: [camera move], stable subject, no rapid cuts.
Avoid: illegible text, logos not supplied, unsupported product claims.
Output use: [organic post / paid ad / landing-page loop].
```

Generate a low-cost draft first when the workspace supports it. Review brand
accuracy, temporal consistency, readable caption space, and rights before any
upscale or final export.

## Ads and publishing preflight

```text
Account and platform:
Selected asset/version:
Objective and conversion event:
Audience and geography:
Budget, billing currency, and schedule:
Landing URL + UTM values:
Copy, disclaimer, and approval owner:

```

Use this checklist before a Windsor.ai write. The operator must explicitly
confirm the exact action, account, budget, and schedule.
